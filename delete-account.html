Delete your account

Applies to Pixel Smash and Pixel Smash Kart

An account is optional — if you never made one, there is nothing to delete and your progress stays on your device. If you did make one, here is how to remove it and everything attached to it.

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open either game and go to Account (on the title screen or from the main menu).
  2. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

This is instant and permanent. It removes your login, username, optional email, save data, friends list, ratings and public profile from our servers. There is no undo and no copy kept.

If you cannot open the game

Email us from the address you used, or tell us your username, and we will start the same deletion for you. We reply to confirm within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Email us to delete your account

What stays on your device

Deleting the account does not touch the save file in your browser or app storage: that copy belongs to you and works offline. To wipe it as well, clear the site data for the game in your browser, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.

Questions

Anything unclear about this, or a deletion that looks incomplete: support@greenlightstudios.app. See also our privacy policy.
