One save. Both games.

Pixel Smash and Pixel Smash Kart share a single save and one online username. Copy a save code (PXSAVE1:…) from the Save data menu in either game to carry your Power and VR to another browser, the downloaded build, or your phone.

Your save also holds a 10-digit friend code that works in both games. Share it, or add players straight from the results screen. Friends show their VR and Power, and both games keep your win–loss record against each one.

Browser build

  1. Download the .html file.
  2. Double-click it. It opens in your browser.
  3. Play. Saves stay on that computer.

Android

  1. Download the .apk on your phone.
  2. Open it and allow installs from your browser when asked.
  3. Tap Install. The game launches fullscreen.

Good to know

  1. Keyboard, gamepad and touch all work. Hide the on-screen controls in Options.
  2. Online play and fonts need internet.
  3. Move saves with a save code.
