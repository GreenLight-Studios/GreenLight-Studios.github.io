About the studio

Greenlight Studios

Two free pixel games that open in a browser: a platform fighter and a kart racer. We made them because the games we wanted to play didn't exist yet.

Why these games

There is a market for small, quick arcade games, and it was mostly empty. What's out there is built for a console, wrapped in a launcher, or asks you to make an account before you can press play. So we made the two games we wanted to be able to open in a tab.

Both are free to play here, with no account and no install. Online play and friends are there if you want them, and nothing is locked behind them: a guest gets the whole game.

What the two games share

  • One file. Grab the .html build and it plays offline, on any machine with a browser.
  • One save. Power, VR, your friend code and your friends follow you between both games, and move to another device with a save code.
  • One version. Both games ship together, so an online match never meets a player on a different build.
  • No ads in the games. The downloaded builds and the Android app are ad-free. This website, not the games, is what pays for itself.
