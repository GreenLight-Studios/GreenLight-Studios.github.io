Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

We don't run accounts or servers, and we don't collect your personal information. This website shows ads from Google AdSense, which uses cookies. The games you download, and the Android app, have no ads and no tracking.

Game saves

Pixel Smash and Pixel Smash Kart keep your progress, settings, username, friend code and friends list in your browser's local storage, on your device. It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so copy a save code first if you want to keep it.

Online play and friends

Online and LAN play connect your device directly to other players (WebRTC). To find each other, the games use the public PeerJS matchmaking server. That server, and the players you connect to, can see your IP address, as with any online game. Your username, friend code, fighter or racer and rating are shared with the players you meet. We don't receive or store any of it.

Ads and cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this website and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on those visits.

  • You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, or turn off many third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time.

The games themselves never show ads on this website. If you play our games on another site, such as CrazyGames or Poki, that site's own privacy policy applies to the ads it shows.

Children

Our games and this website are made for players aged 13 and over.
